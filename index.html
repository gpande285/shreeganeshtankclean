About Us

Since 2007, SHRI GANESH ENTERPRISES has been on a mission to deliver "Clean Tank, Safe Water, Healthy Life" to every household and organization. Founded by Mr. Shriram A. Kaluke in Pune, our company has grown from a small local service to a professionally managed enterprise trusted by thousands of satisfied customers across Pune and PCMC.

With over 17 years of experience, we specialize in Underground and Overhead Water Tank Cleaning Services using Kranzle German-made high-pressure machines, government-approved cleaning chemicals, and UV light disinfection technology.
